## Corpus cross-check (`data/cable-extract/all-dates.ndjson`)

- **All 39 MRNs present**, one record each; `dtg.datetime_iso` matches the paper's dates in all but one case.
- **fn 49 `1976KABUL01765`**: dtg is 1976-**03-09** (cable's own DTG `R 090700Z MAR 76`), paper prints "3 March 1976" — paper appears to have mis-transcribed the day.
- **fn 64 `1979STATE252292`**: no dtg in the record; `dates.draft_date` = 1979-09-25, matching the paper's 25 Sep 1979.
- **fn 47 `1974STATE226822`**: paper prints "15 October **1947**" — typo; corpus dtg confirms 1974-10-15.
- Date field note: the corpus's date namespace changed — for 1973-76 the draft date / dtg is authoritative; 1977+ records carry `dtg.datetime_iso` (used here) plus `sent_date`/`filing_datetime_iso`.

## Verification against the PDF text

- All **39 MRNs** were re-checked against the PDF's text extracted with an independent mode (`pdftotext -raw`, line-wrap aware): all 39 present, exactly as reconstructed. No MRN was missed or mis-transcribed; several are printed hyphen-wrapped across lines (e.g. `1973STATE-049755`, `1974KA-BUL07553`) and join cleanly.
- The flagged items are **genuine features of the printed page** (both `-layout` and `-raw` agree), not OCR artifacts:
  - fn 47 prints "15 October **1947**" (typo for 1974 — dtg 1974-10-15 confirms).
  - fn 49 prints "**3** March 1976" (cable's own DTG is 09 MAR 76).
- The "no-MRN" cables genuinely lack numbers in the paper: fn 50 prints "…22 July 1976, 1976GENEVA,"; fn 59 prints "…4 April 1978, 1978KABUL," and "…13 April 1978;"; fn 42 prints "…12 October 1972, 'Narcotics-Views of Afghan Foreign Minister,' RG 59, INM CF, box 3, Afghanistan 1972, NA."
