# Gutfeld & Vanetik (2016), “A Situation That Had to Be Manipulated” — cable findings

**Source:** Arnon Gutfeld and Boaz Vanetik, “A Situation That Had to Be
Manipulated: The American Airlift to Israel During the Yom Kippur War,”
*Middle Eastern Studies* 52(3): 419–447. DOI:
[10.1080/00263206.2016.1144393](https://doi.org/10.1080/00263206.2016.1144393).

**Zotero:** item `6HWEE6H7`, PDF `75BVYQGF`.

The article contains four individually numbered cable/telegram references.
Three resolve to records in the 1973 corpus; one Cairo telegram is not
present. Two of the resolved Saudi cables are cited from Nixon Presidential
Materials rather than CFPF, so their corpus matches are recorded with that
provenance caveat.

## Method and caveats

- MRNs were reconstructed from the year, post, and printed cable number, then
  checked against `data/cable-extract/1973.ndjson`.
- FRUS documents, WSAG minutes, telephone transcripts, memoranda, and other
  numbered documents were not treated as cables unless the article explicitly
  identified them as a cable or telegram.
- `1973JIDDA04549` exists in the corpus metadata but has no readable subject
  or message body in the available extraction; it remains a candidate rather
  than a content-verified match.
