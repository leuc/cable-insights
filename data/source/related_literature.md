# Related literature on the 1970s State Department cable corpus

A curated bibliography for insight lookup — cross-checking findings,
borrowing method framing, or citing prior work. No files from these
citations are checked into this repo; each is a published, independently
locatable work (DOI/URL given). Split into two groups by how closely the
work's data matches this repo's corpus.

## Group A — same dataset: State Dept cables, 1970s, Central Foreign Policy
Files (CFPF)

These work directly with the same underlying corpus this repo's `acp-127`
pipeline extracts from (NARA's declassified CFPF electronic telegrams,
1973-1979) — either analyzing it at bulk/computational scale or describing
its construction. Best candidates for cross-checking corpus-level findings
(e.g. volume, classification, topic patterns) or citing as prior art.

- Chaney, A., Wallach, H., Connelly, M., & Blei, D. (2016). *Detecting and
  Characterizing Events*. Proceedings of EMNLP 2016.
  https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/D16-1122
  — "corpus of over two million U.S. State Department cables from the
  1970s," same scale/era as this corpus.
- Connelly, M., Fabius, N., & Hicks, R. *Trade and Economic Diplomacy*.
  (no DOI on file) — FOIArchive-team export-promotion study on 1970s State
  cables.
- Casler, D., Connelly, M., & Hicks, R. (2024). *Trading with Frenemies: How
  Economic Diplomacy Affects Exports*. International Studies Quarterly,
  68(3), sqae098. https://doi.org/10.1093/isq/sqae098
  — "large collection of U.S. State Department cables from the 1970s."
- Connelly, M. J., Hicks, R., Jervis, R., Spirling, A., & Suong, C. H.
  (2021). *Diplomatic documents data for international relations: the
  Freedom of Information Archive Database*. Conflict Management and Peace
  Science, 38(6), 762-781. https://doi.org/10.1177/0738894220930326
  — describes the FOIArchive database that indexes this same CFPF corpus
  (plus other archives); useful for metadata/provenance context.
- Scarpa, F. (2024). *Danish State Interests as Foundation for Greenland
  Home Rule of 1979: A Study of the US Central Foreign Policy Files
  (1973–1979)*. The Yearbook of Polar Law Online, 15(1), 84-117.
  https://doi.org/10.1163/22116427_015010005
  — explicitly "US Central Foreign Policy Files (1973–1979)," exact name
  and date range match.
- Adams, M. (2009). *Sources of Historical International U.S. Data*.
  https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.3781919
  — describes NARA's "electronic telegrams, 1973-1975, from the U.S.
  Department of State's Central Foreign Policy Files," the archive's own
  provenance description.
- Costigliola, F., Hogan, M., Allen, D., & Connelly, M. (2016). *Diplomatic
  history after the big bang: using computational methods to explore the
  infinite archive*. Book chapter.
  https://doi.org/10.1017/CBO9781107286207.006
  — computational-methods overview of this same archive, by the
  FOIArchive PI.
- Souza, R. R., Coelho, F. C., Shah, R., & Connelly, M. (2016). *Using
  Artificial Intelligence to Identify State Secrets*. arXiv:1611.00356.
  https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1611.00356
  — "nearly a million State Department cables from the 1970s"; already used
  as a cross-check in
  [`questions/dash-counter-meaning`](../../questions/dash-counter-meaning/HYPOTHESIS.md).

## Group B — mentions cables as historical evidence, not the bulk corpus

Diplomatic-history / IR case studies and theory pieces that cite individual
State Department cables (often CFPF-sourced via NARA's AAD) as
primary-source evidence for a narrative argument, rather than analyzing the
corpus at scale. Useful for substantive/historical context (what a finding
might *mean* diplomatically) rather than corpus-level cross-checks.

  (no DOI on file)
- Carboex, Y. (2025). *The Environment in Deep Water: Environmentalists,

  History, 30(1), 116-144. https://doi.org/10.1086/733418
- Weimer, D. (2019). *"It's That Difficult of a Terrain": Opium,
  Development, and Territoriality in US-Afghan Relations, 1940s–1970s*. The
  Social History of Alcohol and Drugs, 33(1), 113-144.
  https://doi.org/10.1086/702695
- Szalontai, B., & Jinil, Y. (2023). *Maneuvering between Baghdad and
  Tehran: North Korea's Relations with Iraq and Iran during the Cold War*.
  Journal of Cold War Studies, 25(2), 179-247.
  https://doi.org/10.1162/jcws_a_01119
- Lee, M. M. (2018). *The International Politics of Incomplete Sovereignty:
