## Corpus cross-check (`data/cable-extract/all-dates.ndjson`)

- **All 61 distinct cables present** (59 in the table above's primary match + `1978MANILA00632` / `1978MANILA00721` from fn 140/141, see notes). `_subject` lines match the paper's footnote titles in every case; `dtg.datetime_iso` matches the paper's dates except where flagged.
- **Year misprints in the paper's footnotes** (body text and context establish the correct year):
  - **fn 140 "Derian… No. 0632, 'Human Rights in the Philippines,' 12 January 1977"** → actual cable is **`1978MANILA00632`** (subject "HUMAN RIGHTS IN THE PHILIPPINES", dtg **12 Jan 1978**). Note: `1977MANILA00632` *does* exist but is an unrelated cable ("LOCK REQUIREMENTS"). Body text (p. 404) dates Derian's visit January 1978.
  - **fn 141 "Newsome… No. 0721, 'Human Rights in the Philippines,' 13 January 1977"** → actual cable is **`1978MANILA00721`** (subject "HUMAN RIGHTS: ASSISTANT SECRETARY DERIAN'S MEETING WITH…", dtg **13 Jan 1978**). No `1977MANILA00721` exists in the corpus.
  - **fn 164 "Murphy… No. 19451, 'Senator Inouye's Visit,' 1 November 1975"** → actual cable is **`1978MANILA19451`** (subject matches, dtg **1 Nov 1978**). "1975" is a typo for 1978 (Inouye's visit was Oct-Nov 1978 per body text).
- **Other flagged items**:
  - **fn 71 misprints "No. 1733"** for the Marcos-Robinson meeting cable; the meeting cable is `1976MANILA11733` (fn 87 cites the correct number).
  - **fn 130 prints "No. 290510CFPF"** — "CFPF" is appended to the number in a formatting slip; MRN is `1977STATE290510`.
  - **fn 130 `1977STATE248525`**: paper says "16 October 1977", dtg is 17 Oct (raw `O 171730Z OCT 77`) — off by one day.
  - **fn 163 `1978STATE259598`**: corpus subject is "TRAVEL OF CODEL INOUYE AND STAFFDEL JORDAN" (paper's title is a paraphrase). Record has dtg 1978-10-21 but `dates.draft_date` 1978-10-13 — internal draft/DTG mismatch; paper's 21 Oct matches the DTG.
  - **fn 165 `1978MANILA19456`**: paper says "5 November 1978", dtg is 01 Nov — off by 4 days.
- **Duplicates in the corpus** (matched in `all-dates.ndjson`, as in the Hulme 2026 extraction):
  - **`1976STATE268153`** appears twice: `1976STATE268153-1.txt` (no dtg; draft 1976-11-09) and `1976STATE268153.txt` (subject "MUTUAL DEFENSE TREATY: AIDE-MEMOIRE"; dtg 09 NOV 76). Same MRN as the duplicate flagged in the Hulme cross-check.
  - **`1976STATE293490`** appears twice: dtg 1976-12-02 and a second record (draft 1976-12-20).
- **`1976STATE399727`** (fn 105, "10 December 1976", tagged NSA/GFPL) is **NOT in the corpus** — likely a misprint: the same fn cites 300727 for 10 Dec, and a State cable numbered 399727 for Dec 1976 is implausible. **`1977STATE875275`** (fn 117, "Presidential Letter to Marcos," 5 Apr 1977, tagged JCPL) is **NOT in the corpus** — number pattern suggests a JCPL/NLC accession, not a CFPF telegram. **`1975MANILA05354`** (fn 52-53, "Proposed Letter from President Ford to President Marcos," 25 Apr 1975, tagged NSA/GFPL) is **NOT in the corpus** — this is the NSC copy of a proposed letter, not a released cable.
- **Out of corpus by type**: fn 57/58 are **airgrams** (A-27, A-99, tagged DNSA) — airgrams are not in the electronic-telegram corpus. fn 122 (Underhill/Kuala Lumpur telegram, "U.S. military bases in the Philippines," 9 Feb 1977) is cited **without a cable number** via FRUS 1977-80 XXII — unresolvable here.

## Content verification against the cable text

Spot-checked the body claims' key phrases against `_message_content` in the per-year files — all present:
- **fn 36 `1975MANILA07891`** (Habib): "NO LONGER ARE THE FILIPINOS PREPARED TO SUFFER THIRD-WORLD ALLEGATIONS OF 'U.S. STOOGE.' NO LONGER ARE THEY WILLING TO BE CONSIDERED THE RETARDED EX-COLONIALS OF SEA."
- **fn 41 `1975MANILA09896`**: "COCOON OF FOREIGN PROTECTION" — cable's framing of leaving the cocoon "AND YET STAY IN IT," matching the paper's "having your cocoon and leaving it, too."
- **fn 83 `1976STATE192534`** (Robinson's instructions): "WE DO NOT BELIEVE IT USEFUL TO TRY TO ENVISAGE HOW THE TREATY WOULD BE APPLIED IN PARTICULAR CIRCUMSTANCES SUCH AS ATTACK IN DISPUTED AREAS (E.G., THE SPRATLYS). OUR REACTION IN SUCH A CASE WOULD DEPEND ENTIRELY…" — quoted verbatim in the paper.
- **fn 126 `1977MANILA15267`** (Holbrooke assessment): "HE HAS FORMULATED AN EQUATION IN WHICH THE MORE CREDIBLE THE SECURITY ASSURANCES THE U.S. CAN OFFER, THE LESS THE MILITARY ASSISTANCE THE PHILIPPINES WILL REQUIRE…" — quoted almost verbatim.

## Notes

- PDF is the **corrected** online version (title page carries the 2021 correction notice DOI 10.1080/07075332.2021.1943134); all pages were read from this PDF. The paper itself is an "originally published with errors" corrected version — some citation slips above may be residual.
- The paper relies heavily on non-corpus archives for the same documents: GFPL (NSA/EAP Subject Files), JCPL (NLC), FRUS 1969-76 E-12 and 1977-80 XXII, DNSA, and CIA PDBs. Only State Dept telegrams present in the CFPF electronic-telegram corpus are tabulated.
- Reconstructed MRNs use the station code in the paper's own sender line ("Sullivan (Manila) to State" → MANILA; "State to Manila" → STATE); for `1976STATE190998`/`1976STATE192534` (Habib/Kissinger "to Canberra") the MRNs are STATE-series, matching the corpus.
