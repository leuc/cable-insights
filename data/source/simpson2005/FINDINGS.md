# Simpson (2005), "'Illegally and Beautifully': The United States, the Indonesian Invasion of East Timor and the International Community, 1974-76" — cable findings

Source: *Cold War History* 5(3): 281-315, https://doi.org/10.1080/14682740500222028. Notes on pp. 305-313.
Simpson cites US State Dept cables by **telegram number + sender + date** (no MRNs). MRN reconstructed as `YYYY + station + 5-digit telegram number` and checked against `data/cable-extract/all-dates.ndjson`. Many were obtained by the author via FOIA and are **NODIS/withheld** cables absent from the AAD electronic-telegrams corpus.

## Notes

- The corpus contains **no Surabaya consulate cables numbered in the 3300s**; the Surabaya run (00127, 00145-147, 00160) is at far lower numbers, so all three Surabaya citations are FOIA-only.
- `1975JAKART01579` (the famous 6 Dec 1975 Ford-Kissinger-Suharto cable) is deliberately absent — it was NODIS and released later via FOIA/NSA, not part of the declassified electronic telegrams.
- Matched cables were validated on MRN + date consistency, with content checked on the key Timor cables (10244, 12275, 252899, 037929, 06284, 05688, 04955, CANBER 7933).
