04Antichain

No stable signal, giant build only

antichain layers cables into a “mainstream” dominant broadcast tier vs. minor parallel chains — testable only on the giant build (n≤52), which lacks the ground-truth coverage to say much either way.

What was tested

Whether cited cables were over- or under-represented in the single most populated antichain layer, vs. that layer's expected share under random draw from the same background pool.

What it found

morley2019 and the pooled group show mild enrichment (33/52 observed vs. 26.2 expected, q=0.135) — not significant, and the opposite direction from an earlier build of this same graph. Treated as no usable lead.

06Caveats

What to weigh before reusing this

Sample size

morley2019 (76 confirmed) and weimer2019 (39) supply most statistical power even in the non-giant build; harmer2013/lee2018 remain too small individually at 4 and 2.

Date confound

Same-year and full-corpus backgrounds gave near-identical results — largely because the ground truth spans nearly the graph's full 1973–79 range, not strong proof the confound is ruled out.

FDR correction

Applied separately per test family (numeric / categorical / cd-index / antichain). Pooling community-leiden into cd-index-type's family in an earlier pass inflated the latter's apparent significance — don't recombine the result CSVs without accounting for this.

Ground truth noise

Source citations carry their own uncertainty (date offsets, topical-fit-only matches). confirmed/flagged tiers are reported separately throughout, not merged silently.
